Our Services

Daily Meditation

Join our daily meditation sessions to cultivate mindfulness and inner peace. Our experienced teachers guide you through various meditation techniques suitable for all levels.

Schedule: Morning 6:00 AM - 7:00 AM, Evening 6:00 PM - 7:00 PM

Dhamma Talks

Weekly teachings on Buddhist principles and practices. Learn from our senior monks about the teachings of Buddha and how to apply them in daily life.

Schedule: Wednesday & Sunday 7:30 PM

Retreats

Extended meditation retreats for deeper spiritual practice. Immerse yourself in intensive practice with our community of practitioners.

Duration: Weekend and week-long retreats available

Community Events

Various events including festivals, workshops, and charity activities. Celebrate Buddhist festivals and participate in meaningful community service.

Special Events: Burmese New Year, Bodhi Day, and more
