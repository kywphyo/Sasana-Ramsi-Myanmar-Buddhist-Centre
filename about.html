About Us

Sasana Ramsi Myanmar Buddhist Centre is a serene monastery dedicated to the practice of Buddhism. We offer a tranquil environment for meditation, learning, and community gatherings.

Our Mission

To provide a sanctuary for spiritual seekers and promote peace, compassion, and wisdom in our community.

About Our Community

Since our establishment, Sasana Ramsi Vihara has been a beacon of spiritual guidance and peaceful practice. We welcome people from all backgrounds to join us in our journey towards enlightenment and inner peace.
